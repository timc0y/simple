## Simple

Invoke `$simple` and read the nearest `SIMPLE.md` before architecture, ownership, compatibility, migration, deletion, or broad refactoring decisions. Also use Simple writing mode for technical plans, documentation, comments, Markdown, prompts, reviews, progress updates, and handoffs that should be concise, plain, and load-bearing. Use `simple emulate` when asked what a documented engineer, company, or operator would do: emulate the sourced decision doctrine, not personality, then synthesize against repository truth. Writing and emulation must not expand into unrelated architecture analysis. Repository facts override speculative compatibility, migration, scale, and operator prestige. Use `simple init`, `audit`, `plan`, `review`, `write`, `emulate`, or `check` for an explicit Simple workflow.
