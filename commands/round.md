Use `$simple` in `round` mode. Read the nearest `SIMPLE.md`, `references/commands.md`
under `simple round`, and `references/writing.md`. The round is `simple/round.md` beside
the profile: create it from the user's ask on the first call, otherwise refresh it and
reply with the state line, the four buckets, and the dated decisions on one screen. With
the argument `close`, move surviving decisions to their durable owners and delete the
file. End with the stop condition, not an offer.
