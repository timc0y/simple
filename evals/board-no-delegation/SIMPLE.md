# Simple

## Reality

- Stage and users: One operator restores customer archives when requested.
- Persistent production data: Off-site archives may outlive the local index.
- Compatibility commitments: Archive retention is set by the owner, not the repository.

## Preserve

- The ability to restore every retained archive.

## Current boundary

- The local index contains no version-one archives.
- The repository cannot inspect the off-site archive inventory or retention promise.

## Ordinary paths

- One decoder restores version-one and version-two archives.

## Proof

- Restore representative retained archives through the public restore command.

## Reconsider when

- Remove the version-one decoder after the owner confirms no retained version-one archive remains.
