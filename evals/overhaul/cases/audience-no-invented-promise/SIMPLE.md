# Simple

## Reality

- The source fact is limited to malformed-row line numbers and a test-dataset result.
- The audience is customers deciding whether to upgrade.
- Product documentation owns the release note.

## Preserve

- The line-number behaviour and the qualification that the observed reduction was in a
  test dataset.

## Current boundary

- The release note may explain the customer relevance but cannot generalize the test
  result into a production guarantee.

## Ordinary paths

- State the shipped behaviour, its practical use, and the evidence boundary plainly.

## Proof

- Check that a customer can understand what changed and that no unsupported production
  performance promise appears.
