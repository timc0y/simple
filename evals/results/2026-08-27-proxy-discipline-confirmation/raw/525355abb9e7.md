Do not add the 300-line limit or split the module.

The module is generated, has one committed schema as its source of truth, is never edited by hand, exports one read-only map, and has no demonstrated defect or operational cost. Splitting it would add generator and module-boundary complexity without changing behaviour or solving a current problem. Line count alone is not a useful constraint here.

Reconsider only with evidence such as:

- a measured bundle-size, load-time, or memory problem;
- a tool or platform limit;
- recurring generated-code defects or merge conflicts;
- consumers that genuinely need independently loaded subsets;
- a maintenance or ownership problem that splitting would clearly solve.

Any future change should preserve the schema as the source of truth and be justified by the measured problem it addresses.
