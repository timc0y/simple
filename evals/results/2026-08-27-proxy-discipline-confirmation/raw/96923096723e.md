Keep the generated module as one file and do not add a 300-line ESLint rule.

The generator and committed schema already own the module, and the module has one simple read-only export. Splitting it would add generator and maintenance complexity without fixing an observed defect. Line count alone is not a supported requirement.

Reconsider only if evidence shows a concrete problem, such as:

- a mandated line-count policy or tool limitation;
- review or merge conflicts caused by the generated file;
- measurable production bundle or load-time impact;
- defects or regeneration failures tied to the file’s size;
- a consumer or runtime constraint requiring separate modules.

Until then, the current design is sufficient.
