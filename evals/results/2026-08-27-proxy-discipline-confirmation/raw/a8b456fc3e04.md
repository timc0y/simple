Do not enable only a 300-line limit. It detects the symptom but leaves tax and email knowledge coupled in `billing.ts`.

Make `billing.ts` the invoice-validation and orchestration boundary:

- Keep invoice validation there.
- Call the existing tax module to calculate tax.
- Call the existing notification module to render billing emails.
- Remove the corresponding tax and email logic from `billing.ts`.

This follows the existing owners and eliminates the shared edit surface that caused the conflicts. A file limit may be added afterward as a guard only if the resulting boundary still needs one; it is not the change that fixes the coupling.

Prove it through `billing`’s public behaviour: representative invoices must produce the same accepted/rejected results, totals, taxes, and rendered emails. Then make independent module tests show that a tax-only change does not alter email snapshots, and an email-only change does not alter tax results. Reconsider the boundary if invoice validation itself depends on tax or rendering policy that cannot be expressed through those modules’ existing interfaces.
