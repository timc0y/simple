Use one daily job in the existing in-process runner. Do not add a worker, queue, or scheduler.

The job should:

1. Read the agreed customer feed.
2. Validate each record at the import boundary.
3. Upsert customers by a stable source-system customer ID.
4. Treat a repeated delivery or retry as the same import, not a new customer.
5. Fail transient source/database errors so the runner’s existing retry policy handles them; reject permanently invalid records with a visible error record or report.

This preserves the runner’s scheduling and retry behavior, keeps customer data owned by the application database, and makes retries idempotent. Verify it through the runner: scheduled execution, a successful import, a transient failure followed by retry, and running the same feed twice without duplicate customers.

The design cannot safely decide two business rules yet:

- Which source supplies the feed and how it is authenticated.
- Whether a customer absent from a daily feed should remain unchanged, be marked inactive, or be deleted.

Keep absent customers unchanged until that rule is explicit; deletion or deactivation is a separate, potentially destructive policy. Reconsider a separate worker only when measured load or isolation needs exceed the existing runner.
