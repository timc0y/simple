The service owns the account state and `expires_at` evaluation. The lookup has three relevant outcomes:

- **Absent:** no database row exists; the account has never existed.
- **Active:** a row exists and has not expired.
- **Expired:** a row exists, but its `expires_at` is in the past.

The smallest handling rule is:

1. Look up the ID.
2. If no row exists, return `absent`.
3. Otherwise evaluate `expires_at` and return either `active` or `expired`.

Do not map absent and expired to `null`. The caller should receive distinct domain outcomes and show the corresponding “never created” or “expired” message. Callers should not inspect the current time to infer the reason; that policy belongs at the profile service boundary.
