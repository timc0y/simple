Filter first, then rank:

1. Keep only destinations where `status = active` and `region = user_region`.
2. Among those eligible destinations, choose the one with the lowest `latency_ms`.
3. If none remain, do not send the notification.

Proof case: for user region `eu-west`, destination A is inactive with 10 ms latency, while destination B is active in `eu-west` with 50 ms latency. A is removed during filtering, so B is selected.
