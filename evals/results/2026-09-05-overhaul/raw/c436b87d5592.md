Filter first, then rank:

1. Keep destinations where `status = active` and `region = user_region`.
2. Select the remaining destination with the lowest `latency_ms`.
3. If none remain, send no notification.

Proof case: with an inactive destination at 10 ms and an active, same-region destination at 50 ms, the 50 ms destination is selected. The faster destination is excluded before ranking, so ineligible destinations can never win.
