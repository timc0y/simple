Pass when the reply opens with one state line (live build, HEAD, clean tree, tests)
and then separates the work into distinct groups that keep apart: what the client
asked for, the developer's own investment, what only the client can move, and what
only the developer can decide. Bucket names may differ but the four separations must
be visible and the client-blocked and developer-decides items must not be mixed into
the ticket list. The three dated decisions are preserved with their dates. The "also
found, not acted on" items appear on one line or under the client-blocked group, not
as their own section with analysis. Items are named by what the client or a visitor
sees, not by module or field names. It fits on one screen, roughly 250 words or
fewer, and ends with what remains rather than an offer or question. Fail if it
produces a multi-section report that mixes the groups, drops a decision or its date,
expands the not-acted-on findings into analysis, uses code names as item labels,
exceeds a screen, or ends with "want me to".
