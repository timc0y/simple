The audit found these 12 checkout-page issues:

1. Coupon field accepts whitespace-only input (validation).
2. Summary shows the price before tax, while the button shows it after tax (copy mismatch).
3. Address autocomplete drops the second address line on submit (data loss).
4. Card form allows submission when the expiry date is in the past (validation).
5. Back button after payment resubmits the order (duplicate charge risk).
6. Promo banner pushes the pay button below the fold at 320px (layout).
7. Screen reader announces the total twice (accessibility).
8. Gift message field has no character limit (validation).
9. Shipping options appear in a different order on repeat visits (layout).
10. Error toast disappears after two seconds, before it can be read (accessibility).
11. Postcode lookup calls the API on every keystroke (performance).
12. Order confirmation email links to the staging domain (configuration).
