# Trial plan

## Remaining

- Use moon as the first dataset to try. The synthetic bench record shows 24 of 24
  tiles valid and a median alignment error of 18 m, compared with Earth’s 15 of 18
  valid and 44 m median error.
- Run the moon field trial and record alignment, runtime and missing tiles. This
  trial is unrun; the bench result does not establish field performance.
