# Dataset references

## Bench evidence

Synthetic bench record M-17: moon had 24 of 24 tiles valid and a median alignment
error of 18 m. Earth had 15 of 18 tiles valid and a median alignment error of 44 m.
This is bench evidence only; it does not establish field performance.

## First trial choice

Moon is now first because it has complete tile validity and the lower median
alignment error in the available bench comparison. Runtime, field alignment and
missing tiles remain unknown until the field trial runs. Field trials have not run.
