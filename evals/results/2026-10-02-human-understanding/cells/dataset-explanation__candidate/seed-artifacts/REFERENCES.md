# Dataset references

## Bench evidence

Measurement M-17 is a synthetic bench record, not a field result:

- Moon: 24 of 24 tiles valid; median alignment error 18 m.
- Earth: 15 of 18 tiles valid; median alignment error 44 m.

Moon is the first dataset to try because it has complete bench tile validity and
the lower median alignment error. This is a bench-based choice, not evidence
that moon will perform better in the field.

## First trial choice

Moon is first. Field trials have not run.

## What remains unknown

The field trial has not measured alignment, runtime or missing tiles for either
dataset. The synthetic bench result does not establish real-dataset performance
or explain whether moon's advantage will hold in the field.
