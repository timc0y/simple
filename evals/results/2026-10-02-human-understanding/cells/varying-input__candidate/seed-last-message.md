## Decision

Under the stated estimate, the review does **not** fit in 45 minutes:

`5 minutes setup + 24 items × 2 minutes = 53 minutes`

That is **8 minutes over** the limit.

| Change | Maximum to fit in 45 minutes |
|---|---:|
| Keep 24 items | Average item time must be **≤ 1 minute 40 seconds** |
| Keep 2 minutes per item | At most **20 items** |
| General rule | `5 + item count × minutes/item ≤ 45` |

Examples:

- 20 items at 2 minutes = 45 minutes: fits exactly.
- 24 items at 1.5 minutes = 41 minutes: fits.
- 24 items at 2 minutes = 53 minutes: does not fit.

The 5-minute setup is measured from a rehearsal. The 2-minute item time is only an assumption; actual item time has not been measured. Therefore, this is a planning decision under the assumption, not proof of an actual review duration. No real review was run. Source: [evidence/review.md]([REDACTED: fixture path]).
