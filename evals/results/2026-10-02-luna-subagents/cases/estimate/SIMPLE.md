# Photo tagging estimate

## Reality
This is an estimate for one operator, not a booking or a measured throughput claim. Setup took 7 minutes in one observation. The per-photo time of 1.75 minutes is assumed and remains unmeasured. The available session is 60 minutes.

## Decision
Compare 24, 30 and 36 photos. Give the operator a way to recompute for another count or per-photo time. A result based on an assumed time does not guarantee completion.
