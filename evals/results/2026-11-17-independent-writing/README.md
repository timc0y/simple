# Independent writing screen, 17 November 2026

Registered before candidate generation at the owner's request. This is a bounded
screen of explicitly supplied writing instructions, not installed-skill activation,
personal voice validation or reader comprehension. No outcome is established yet.

Use `gpt-6-luna`, medium reasoning, through native collaboration in fresh contexts.
The exact backend revision and token usage may not be exposed. Simple's protocol
specifies Luna; use the same model for both repositories to hold that factor fixed.
No paid API inference, private writing, training, installation or publishing.

An independent author who cannot read either skill supplies four new tasks per repo,
source-grounded reader questions and four grader controls. Compare minimal writing
instructions with each repo's current frozen writing instructions. Repeat the
current condition with identical task/method content. Context/task identities still
differ, so this is not an identical host-session replay. Generation receives no
acceptance answers, grading controls or condition mapping.

Budget: one author task, six four-draft batches (two repos by three conditions),
and four blinded grading tasks (two repos by two answer orders): eleven model tasks,
no automatic retries, repairs or tuning. Preserve failures, invalid artifacts and
missing outcomes. Stop at that bound. Paid API call budget: zero.

Assess each source obligation and requested operation before preference. Record
meaning, completeness and use-unchanged/edit/reject judgments separately. A faithful
answer that fails the requested improvement remains unsuccessful. Both presentation
orders must agree to count a preference; retain disagreements. All controls must
receive the expected labels before treating that grader's decisions as calibrated.
The lead inspects every preservation failure and baseline-pass/current-fail pair.

Small sample size, one repeat and same-model reviewers cannot establish a general
quality gain. Case-author independence is from the implementation, not proof of
population representativeness. Synthetic approved examples cannot establish Tim's
personal voice. Exact frozen inputs, output bytes and task/condition mapping are
retained locally until the run finishes; public evidence must stand on its own.

## Result

**No comparative quality gain is established.** All eleven registered model tasks
finished: one case author, six drafting batches and four fresh blind grading batches.
There are 24 drafts and 64 recorded assessments across the two repositories. No
outputs were repaired and no model task was retried. The model alias and reasoning
setting were requested as registered; exact backend revision, token usage and cost
were not exposed and remain unknown.

For Simple, all three conditions changed a material prerequisite:

| Source | All three drafts | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “If it arrives by Tuesday, I can finish the review by Thursday” | “If you send it by Tuesday, I can finish the review by Thursday” | Sending by Tuesday does not ensure arrival by Tuesday. |

Both blind grading orders called those three drafts meaning-preserving and usable
unchanged. They answered the reader question with the source's arrival condition,
even though the draft said sending. Agreement between reviewers did not establish
preservation. The lead's source comparison identifies this concrete error; it is not
an independent human-reader study.

The forward grading artifact also contains a non-literal draft quotation. The reverse
artifact labels a one-item preference as a tie. Neither artifact is structurally
valid under the registered judgment contract. Both match only two of four controls
on all three assessment labels. Some control disagreement is definitional: a reviewer
may reasonably call an omitted fallback both changed meaning and incomplete content.
That calibration ambiguity must remain separate from the indisputable quotation and
preference-shape defects. No control labels were changed after grading.

The two no-op cases across the run stayed byte-identical to their sources in every
condition. Two of
four Simple current/repeat outputs were byte-identical; the other two varied. These
observations cannot rescue an invalid comparison or support an average improvement.
Diagnostic counters in [analysis](evidence/analysis.json) are not quality scores.

## Evidence and limits

[Cases](evidence/cases.json), [minimal drafts](evidence/drafts-minimal.json),
[current drafts](evidence/drafts-current.json), [repeat drafts](evidence/drafts-repeat.json),
[forward judgments](evidence/grader-forward.json), [reverse judgments](evidence/grader-reverse.json),
[condition key](evidence/condition-key.json), [run identities](evidence/run.json) and
[hash manifest](evidence/manifest.json) are retained. The evidence directory also
contains exact generation and grading inputs, the original authored controls, and
pre-generation control corrections. It is synthetic public evidence, not private
conversation history.

The current method was frozen before the new tasks were authored. An independent
helper was instructed not to read the skills or old cases. Helpers had fresh
conversation contexts but shared filesystem access; isolation was cooperative, not
an enforced held-out-data boundary. Graders used the same model alias as writers,
not an independent model family. Gold answers were visible to graders, so this run
cannot establish that their question answers were derived independently from drafts.

These are four short Simple tasks and four Communicator tasks. The exception case
is not a long-document test. Closely related synthetic voice examples do not isolate
style learning from content reuse. No installed skill activation, automatic library
meaning-review operation, real personal voice, external truth or reader comprehension
was tested. Interface Craft and Parallax were not writing treatments.

Stop at the registered bound. Retain the requested writing guidance; do not claim
that it improved performance or remove it on this small screen. Before a later
comparison, distinguish source-faithful omission from task completion consistently,
and obtain draft-only question answers before revealing source answers. This run
exposes those evaluation limits; it does not validate that proposed repair.
