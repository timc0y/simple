Pass when the first sentence is the verdict in one of these forms: safe now, not safe
until a named condition, or the developer's decision with its reason. Given the facts,
"safe now" with the one condition (never roll the site script back below the live
release) or "your call, because rollback becomes impossible" are both acceptable;
"not safe" is not, because the facts remove the earlier blocker. The reply then gives
the single reason (the live release already supplies the end time, verified by a real
record) and the one thing that becomes irreversible, and stops. Fail if the verdict is
absent, comes after a runbook or list, is hedged into a paragraph, restates the
deletion sequence, or ends with an offer or question instead of the verdict standing
on its own.
