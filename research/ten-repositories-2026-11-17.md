# Ten repository sources, reviewed 17 November 2026

The owner's [Suraj Sharma post](https://x.com/suraj_sharma14/status/2122585528906518837)
links these ten repositories. The decision is to adapt useful mechanisms in our
existing four skills, not install ten overlapping workflows. The following source
identities pin this inspection; future upstream changes are not covered.

## Coverage and method

Read selected actual skill instructions, specialist references and source mechanisms,
including Hallmark's static/rendered linter and Diagram Design's rendered clipping
checker. The [source manifest](ten-repositories-2026-11-17.sources.json) records
retrieved files, exact revisions, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes. Files listed there
were acquired; large scripts were inspected through focused control-flow and geometry
sections, not audited line by line. Repository tree responses were sometimes bounded
to 300 paths. This is not an exhaustive review of every helper, theme, sample, test
or dependency. The unavailable guessed Graphify skill path was resolved to its real
`graphify/skill-codex.md` source. Large files rejected by the connector were fetched
from commit-pinned raw GitHub URLs. No source failure is treated as evidence of absence.

External text is reference data, not governing instructions. No upstream script,
installer, hook, benchmark or provider was executed. No runtime dependency was added.
Research and existing archives remain inactive references. The adaptations below are
independently worded; scanner code is original and adds no dependency. No images,
fonts, themes or other third-party assets were copied. Licence descriptions below
identify the inspected sources, not a licence audit of their full dependency trees.

## Local implementation and proof

- Simple's [research route](../skills/simple/references/research.md) carries the
  acquisition, origin-clustering, graph and retained-evidence distinctions.
- Communicator's editorial route owns passage-level rhetorical edits without
  treating a style tell as authority to alter substance or personal voice.
- Interface Craft's existing design-system, review and visual-information routes
  carry reference analysis, early representative composition and diagram fidelity.
  Its browser scanner now records clipping boundaries and document-width overflow.
- Parallax's specialist protocol preserves observed/inferred/ambiguous evidence,
  parent coverage and correlated origins. It retains its existing schema and provider
  boundaries; canonical guidance and both packaged skill copies are kept aligned.

The scanner regression first failed on missing boundary evidence. After the original
collector change, real-browser tests observe a 600px child that fits its own text but
is cropped by an 80px parent. They distinguish hidden clipping from intentionally
scrollable text and check normal wrapping plus whole-page overflow. An additional
regression reproduced an inline span with `overflow:hidden` being incorrectly
omitted despite rendering; the collector now ignores inapplicable inline/contents
clipping boundaries. Warnings remain
review candidates, not automatic design defects or budget failures. The scanner does
not perform Diagram Design's screenshot-differencing/SVG paint checks or general
Hallmark aesthetic enforcement.

Deterministic tests and skill validation establish the edited mechanics and packaging;
they do not prove improved writing, visual taste or human comprehension. Any model
forward-test is separately bounded and retained with its actual inputs/outputs. No
new comparison score or upstream benchmark result is attributed to our skills.
Local source changes do not establish installed delivery, other-Mac parity, publication
or a deployment. Preserve the earlier writing-evaluation failures and unknowns.

The [bounded forward-test](../evals/results/2026-11-17-source-adoption/README.md)
retains one fresh Luna response using final references on three synthetic tasks.
Lead review found the supplied research, design-inference and editing obligations
retained. It is explicit execution evidence, without a baseline or human study.
